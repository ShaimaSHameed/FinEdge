A predictive AI platform for stock market analysis. Pulls live market data through APIs and runs three models across 237 US large-cap stocks. All three feed into a combined output giving a Buy, Sell, or Hold recommendation. The fundamental model outperformed the market by 12 percentage points over a 10-year backtest.

**My role:** Project Manager and Fundamental Analysis Lead, team of 4
